## How We Think About It

Every company I work with ends up building the same four layers, in the same order. Some need all four. Some need one. Either way, it helps to know where you stand.

<div class="row g-4 my-2">
  <div class="col-md-6 col-lg-3">
    <div class="card h-100 p-4">
      <h3 id="layer-data-foundation" class="h5">1. Data Foundation</h3>
      <p class="mb-0">Every system's data in one place your company owns and controls.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="col-md-6 col-lg-3">
    <div class="card h-100 p-4">
      <h3 id="layer-controls" class="h5">2. Controls</h3>
      <p class="mb-0">Automated, preventive controls built into the data, so management reviews are real reviews instead of weeks of gathering and reconciling.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="col-md-6 col-lg-3">
    <div class="card h-100 p-4">
      <h3 id="layer-reporting" class="h5">3. Reporting</h3>
      <p class="mb-0">Reports built on reconciled data, so everyone works from the same numbers.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="col-md-6 col-lg-3">
    <div class="card h-100 p-4">
      <h3 id="layer-intelligence" class="h5">4. Intelligence</h3>
      <p class="mb-0">AI working on approved, reconciled data, so its answers come from your real numbers.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<p><strong>Run it side by side. Tie it out. Then rely on it.</strong> Nothing new replaces an existing process until it has run beside it for a full close and the numbers match.</p>

<p><strong>Your data belongs to you.</strong> We build your warehouse and lakehouse in your own Microsoft tenant, or host it for you in a dedicated environment until you're ready to take it over. Either way, it's your data, and it moves with you.</p>

## Where Companies Start

<div class="card p-4 mb-4">
  <h3 id="readiness" class="h4 mb-2">AI &amp; Data Readiness Snapshot and Assessment</h3>
  <p class="text-body-secondary small mb-3">For CEOs and leadership teams · Snapshot: 3–4 weeks · Full Assessment: 8–12 weeks</p>
  <p>You know AI matters, your data is spread across a stack of subscriptions, and you're not sure where to start. I map what you have today, every system, what data it holds, how that data moves or doesn't, and what it costs you, then lay out a plan across the four layers with phases, rough costs, and quick wins.</p>
  <p>The <strong>Snapshot</strong> is built for owner-led companies that want a clear plan fast. The full <strong>Assessment</strong> covers the whole company, including a complete controls assessment, and ends with a future-state design and a presentation to your leadership team or board.</p>
  <p><strong>The real story:</strong> for a publicly traded investment firm, we spent three months on the full Assessment, delivered with our data-engineering partner. I led the process and controls work: mapping every accounting process with the people who run it, assessing each control, and designing the controls and evidence for the future state. Together we designed a governed data foundation, a reporting layer, and an intelligence layer where Claude reads approved, reconciled data.</p>
  <a href="/readiness-assessment/" class="btn btn-primary" style="width: fit-content;">See how the Assessment works →</a>
</div>

<div class="card p-4 mb-4">
  <h3 id="prototype-sprint" class="h4 mb-2">AI Prototype Sprint</h3>
  <p class="text-body-secondary small mb-3">For CEOs who want to see AI work on a real problem · Typically eight weekly sessions</p>
  <p>Pick one problem that matters, like scheduling, capacity planning, quoting, or reporting. We meet every week with you and your team and build a working tool on your own data. At the end, your team has used it, and you know exactly what a full custom application would take.</p>
  <p><strong>The real story:</strong> a custom door manufacturer and I built a capacity-planning prototype over eight weekly sessions with the CEO and production team. It worked well enough that it turned into a second engagement to develop it toward a full custom application.</p>
  <a href="/contact/" class="fw-semibold">Let's talk about your problem →</a>
</div>

<div class="card p-4 mb-4">
  <h3 id="own-your-data" class="h4 mb-2">Own Your Data: SaaS Consolidation and Data Foundation</h3>
  <p class="text-body-secondary small mb-3">For companies paying for several systems that don't share data well · Phased build</p>
  <p>Most growing companies collect subscriptions: one for accounting, one for the warehouse, one for the marketplaces, one for planning. Each vendor holds a piece of your data, and every renewal costs more. I build one data foundation you own, then replace subscriptions one at a time with a platform designed around how your business works. Nothing goes live until it has been proven against the old systems.</p>
  <p><strong>The real story:</strong> a specialty retailer replaced NetSuite, Rithum, NetStock, OZ WMS, and its point-of-sale subscription with one platform it owns, after running it side by side with NetSuite for three monthly closes.</p>
  <a href="/case-studies/specialty-retailer-ai-finance/" class="fw-semibold">Read the case study →</a>
</div>

<div class="card p-4 mb-4">
  <h3 id="ai-finance-operations" class="h4 mb-2">AI Finance Operations</h3>
  <p class="text-body-secondary small mb-3">For CFOs and controllers · Built in phases, or one piece at a time</p>
  <p>Books that update as transactions happen. A month-end close measured in days instead of weeks. An AI assistant that reads vendor invoices, enters them, and matches them to purchase orders and receipts, and never approves or pays a bill. A morning outlook for the CEO with current sales, margin, and a forecast for the month. Every piece is built with the controls an auditor expects to see.</p>
  <p><strong>The real story:</strong> the same retailer now posts sales, inventory, and bank activity automatically and closes the month in one day.</p>
  <a href="/case-studies/specialty-retailer-ai-finance/" class="fw-semibold">Read the case study →</a>
