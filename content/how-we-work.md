## 1. It Starts with a Conversation

Every engagement begins with a free scoping call. I want to understand your business, what's slowing you down, and what you've already tried. If I'm not the right fit, I'll tell you.

## 2. Fixed-Fee Engagements

Once we agree on the scope, you get a fixed-fee quote. No hourly billing and no surprise invoices. That covers [readiness assessments](/readiness-assessment/), [prototype sprints](/solutions/#prototype-sprint), and phased builds, each scoped to your company.

## 3. Ongoing Partnership

After a project, many companies keep me on call as a **fractional AI and data partner**: someone who already knows their data, their platform, and their controls. We scope it to what your company needs.

## 4. How We Build

- **Run it side by side. Tie it out. Then rely on it.** Nothing replaces an existing process until it has run beside it for a full close and the numbers match.
- **Nothing gets ripped out.** New systems sit beside the ones you already own until they've earned their place.
- **Year-end is protected.** Build first, rely later.
- **Approved in stages.** Larger builds are split into phases, and each phase is priced at its own approval, based on what the previous phase actually took.
- **Running costs stated up front,** including ongoing support, so there are no surprises after go-live.
