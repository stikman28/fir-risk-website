# FIR Risk Advisory

> Verified, board-readable risk intelligence for security and risk leaders — free, no paywalls. FIR Risk reads major threat reports (Verizon DBIR, Microsoft, CrowdStrike, Mandiant, ENISA, and more) end-to-end, verifies every statistic against the primary source, names vendor incentives before quoting vendor claims, and publishes the findings as weekly newsletters and short intelligence briefs.

Publisher: FIR Risk Advisory (Bruce Bird), https://firrisk.ai
Contact: hello@firrisk.ai
Full text of every edition: https://firrisk.ai/llms-full.txt
All content on this site is free to read, cite, and quote with attribution to FIR Risk Advisory and a link to the source page.
